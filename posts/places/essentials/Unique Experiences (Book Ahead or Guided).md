---
title: Unique Experiences (Book Ahead or Guided)
---
- **Day Trips from Quito**
    - **Cotopaxi Volcano** – Guided excursions (hiking, biking, horseback) must be booked. National Park requires entrance registration.
    - **Mindo Cloud Forest** – Birdwatching, butterfly farms, waterfalls, and chocolate tours. Reserve canopy zipline tours ahead.
    - **Papallacta Hot Springs** – Book spa treatments or private pools early.
    - **Otavalo Market (Saturdays)** – Arrange transport/tour in advance, especially if combining with Cuicocha Lake.
        
- **Adventure Options**
    
    - **Quilotoa Lagoon** – Turquoise crater lake, best with a guide if you want to do the hike loop.
    - **Pasochoa Wildlife Reserve** – Guided hikes for unique páramo landscapes and birdlife.
        
- **Cultural/Hands-On**
    
    - **Cooking Classes** – Learn to make empanadas, ceviche, or locro. Book in La Floresta.
    - **Chocolate Workshops** – Try Pacari or Yumbos; reserve spots for guided tastings.
    - **Local Artisanal Workshops** – Pottery, weaving, or wood carving in nearby towns (e.g., San Antonio de Ibarra).
