## The ones I'd circle first

**Friday:** **Cachita López @ Lyric** is the culturally special, only-tonight choice; **Faena + OBSESSION Miami Spice (10:30 only, $200/pp)** is the more glamorous complete date night. If timing allowed, the **Café La Trova × Fontainebleau takeover** is another unusually good one-night-only option.

**Saturday before the boat (after 5 PM):** **Crandon Golf** if you have several hours; brunch at **KYU** (Wynwood), **Kojin 2.0** (Gables foodie sleeper, Sat only), **Level 6 Rooftop** (Grove Bay views), or **Schnebly** (Homestead — if boat is way south). Afternoon open — **Deuce 100th** / **Andrew** from ~4.

**Saturday night if you're still in Miami after the boat:** **Viva Brazil @ Bandshell** would be my pick for you.

**Dinner:** rank **LPM → Claudie → Cotoa → Azabu**, with **Claudie** moving to #1 if you specifically want **dinner + entertainment** rather than food alone.
