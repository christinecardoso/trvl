---
title: Must Visit Places
---


- **Centro Histórico Highlights**
    
    - **Iglesia de la Compañía de Jesús** – Gold-covered baroque masterpiece (buy ticket in advance online to skip queues).
        
    - **San Francisco Monastery** – 16th-century church with a plaza perfect for people-watching.
        
    - **Museo de la Ciudad** – History of Quito in a former hospital.
        
- **Modern Quito**
    
    - **[[Parque La Carolina]]** – The “Central Park of Quito,” with botanical gardens, orchid house, and the interactive Yaku Water Museum.
        
    - **Guápulo Neighborhood** – Artsy hillside neighborhood with a colonial church and stunning views.
        
- **Scenic Spots**
    
    - **TelefériQo** – Go early morning for clear skies. Can hike to Rucu Pichincha summit (requires good fitness and altitude adjustment).
        
    - **El Panecillo** – Safer to visit by taxi/ride app than walking. Best at sunset.
