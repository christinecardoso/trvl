Shortlist for a Palm Beach / West Palm birthday or weekend night out:

- **[[Okechobee Steakhouse]]** — VIP birthday sirloin tradition; book ahead on the day.
- **Cafe Med** — Happy hour 3:30–5:30 daily (see [[Birthday Eats]]).
- **Grato / The Blue Door / Cholo Soy Cantina** — Reliable birthday-circuit options on the map.
- **Cafe Boulud / Oceano** — Dressier Palm Beach picks when you want a splurge.
