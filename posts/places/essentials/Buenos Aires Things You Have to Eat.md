City-side of the Argentina food list — late dinners, markets, bodegones, and the dedicated beef night.

## Don't miss

- Choripán (especially around San Telmo)
- Empanadas
- Family-style **parrilla** night: provoleta → chorizo → mollejas → entraña → ojo de bife → asado de tira
- Bodegón: milanesa napolitana, ñoquis, ravioli, flan con dulce de leche
- Alfajores, dulce de leche, artisanal gelato

Dinner at **9:30–10:30 PM** is normal. Pair the beef night with an intimate tango / milonga rather than the biggest tourist show.
