- **Equator Experiences**
    - **Mitad del Mundo Monument** – Tourist classic, but still fun.
    - **Intiñan Solar Museum** – More scientific and interactive, worth the small fee.
        
- **Chiva Night Bus**
    - Open-air bus with live music and dancing, a festive way to see the city lights. Needs tickets in advance (especially on weekends).
        
- **Traditional Medicine (Curandería)**
    - Visit Mercado de Santa Clara for a limpia (herbal cleansing ritual).
        
- **Festivals (Timing Check)**
    - If you’re in late August, check for Quito arts/cultural festivals. December 6 is Quito’s foundation celebration (huge parties).
