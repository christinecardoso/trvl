- **Locro de Papa** – A rich potato and cheese soup, often topped with avocado. Quito’s comfort food.
- **Llapingachos** – Potato patties stuffed with cheese, served with peanut sauce.
- **Hornado** – Slow-roasted pork, usually found in markets (Mercado Central, Mercado Iñaquito).
- **Ceviche Quiteño** – Made with shrimp or chochos (Andean lupini beans), served with popcorn and plantain chips.
- **Empanadas de Viento** – Airy, fried empanadas filled with cheese and dusted with sugar.
- **Fresh Fruit & Juices** – Naranjilla, babaco, tree tomato, guanábana—try juices at local markets.
- **Canelazo** – A hot spiced cinnamon drink with aguardiente, perfect for chilly Quito nights.

## 🍲 Must-Eats (Beyond the Basics)

- **Street Food**
    - **Sanduche de Pernil** – Slow-cooked pork sandwiches (La Ronda and street stalls near Parque El Ejido).
    - **Tripas Mishqui** – Grilled cow intestines, seasoned and surprisingly delicious (a late-night classic).
    - **Choclo con Queso** – Giant Andean corn with salty fresh cheese.
        
- **Sweet Treats**
    - **Helados de Paila** – Traditional hand-stirred fruit ice cream made in copper pans (try in La Ronda).
    - **Dulce de Higos con Queso** – Candied figs with fresh cheese.
        
- **Markets for Foodies**
    - **[[places/ecuador/eat/Mercado Central, Quito]]** – Famous for corvina frita (fried fish) and jugo de mora (blackberry juice).
    - **[[Mercado Iñaquito]]** – The best place to try hornado and fresh juices.
        
- **High-End Dining**
    - **Urko** – Seasonal tasting menus focused on Ecuador’s diverse regions. Book in advance.
    - **Zazu** – One of Quito’s most renowned fine-dining restaurants, reservation required.
    - **Casa Gangotena Tea Time** – Historic mansion hotel offering a refined afternoon tea with Ecuadorian twists.

# 🌟 Must-Try Foods by Region

- **Quito:** Locro de papa, empanadas de viento, canelazo (spiced aguardiente drink).
- **Cayambe:** Bizcochos, queso de hoja, hot chocolate.
- **Papallacta:** Fresh trout, quinoa soup.
- **Otavalo:** Indigenous stews, mote (hominy), traditional sweets.
- **On the road:** Helados de paila (fruit ice cream), street roasted choclo con queso.
