Great question — since you’ll be in **Quito the last week of August**, you’re hitting one of the **best times of year for the Andes**. August is dry season (May–September), which means:

- **Clear skies** for volcano and mountain views.
    
- **Little rainfall** compared to other months.
    
- **Cool but pleasant** temps in Quito (~50s–70s °F / 10–21 °C).
    
- Perfect conditions for hiking, markets, and day trips.
    

Here are all the **top options to consider this time of year** — grouped by theme:

---

## 🌆 Quito City Highlights (Anytime, but especially nice in dry season)

- **Historic Old Town (UNESCO site)**: colonial plazas, gilded churches, Calle La Ronda at night.
    
- **Basilica del Voto Nacional**: climb towers for views.
    
- **TelefériQo cable car**: clear August skies = best chance to see Cotopaxi & surrounding volcanoes.
    
- **El Panecillo**: Virgin statue + sweeping views.
    
- **Museums & art**: Capilla del Hombre (Guayasamín), Museo Nacional del Ecuador.
    

---

## 🌋 Andean Nature & Adventure (Best in August’s dry weather)

- **Cotopaxi National Park**: crisp skies for volcano photos, hikes around Limpiopungo Lake.
    
- **Quilotoa Crater Lake**: stunning turquoise lagoon — best visibility in August.
    
- **Antisana Ecological Reserve**: less crowded, condors often spotted.
    
- **Pululahua Crater** (30 min from Quito): unique, inhabited volcanic crater.
    

---

## 🌿 Cloud Forest & Hot Springs

- **Mindo Cloud Forest**: birdwatching, orchids, waterfalls, tubing, and chocolate tours. Lush year-round, but August is drier, so trails are easier.
    
- **Papallacta Hot Springs**: clear Andean skies make soaking outdoors especially magical in late August.
    

---

## 🧵 Markets & Culture

- **Otavalo Market** (2 hrs north): Saturday market is the biggest, but open daily. Dry season makes travel easy and views of nearby mountains (Imbabura, Cotacachi) spectacular.
    
- **Cuicocha Lake** nearby: great for short hikes and boat rides.
    
- **Indigenous villages** along the route: weaving, woodcarving, leatherwork.
    

---

## 🏞 Longer Options (if you add 2–3 days after the wedding)

- **Baños de Agua Santa** (3.5 hrs): waterfalls route, adventure sports, hot springs.
    
- **Amazon Basin (Tena or Coca)**: technically accessible year-round, but August is slightly less rainy than peak wet months.
    
- **Galápagos Islands**: August = cooler water, great for diving/snorkeling with sea lions, penguins, rays. (Requires a flight, so better if you tack on 4–5 days).
    

---

## ✅ Best Picks for August (shortlist)

1. **Cotopaxi** — clear volcano views + Andean hikes.
    
2. **Quilotoa** — most striking crater lake in dry season light.
    
3. **Mindo** — cloud forest adventure, easier trails than rainy months.
    
4. **Papallacta** — hot springs under crisp night skies.
    
5. **Otavalo & Cuicocha** — vibrant market + scenic crater lake.
