Luxury eco-lodge in the Chocó cloud forest, known for incredible biodiversity, aerial gondola rides, and guided nature experiences
<!--more-->
Mashpi Lodge is a luxury eco-lodge hidden in Ecuador’s Chocó cloud forest, offering biodiversity tours, gondola rides, and sustainable luxury near Quito.

- Nestled deep in the Chocó cloud forest northwest of Quito, Mashpi Lodge is a world-class eco-lodge
- offering luxury accommodations amidst one of the most biodiverse regions on earth. Guests can
- experience guided hikes, night walks, waterfalls, birdwatching, and the unique Dragonfly gondola.
- The lodge blends sustainability with comfort, making it a top choice for travelers seeking both
- adventure and relaxation in nature.
