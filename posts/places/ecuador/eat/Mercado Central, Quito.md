## Schedule
Explore Mercado Central or Iñaquito for local food and daily life

**Lunch** — Immerse in local flavors at **[[places/ecuador/eat/Mercado Central, Quito|Mercado Central, Quito]]**: **hornado** (slow-roasted pork with mote), **locro de papa** (potato soup), **jugo de mora** (blackberry juice). *Quieter alt:* **San Ignacio Cafetería**.

*Tip: Carry cash for markets and small cafés.*
