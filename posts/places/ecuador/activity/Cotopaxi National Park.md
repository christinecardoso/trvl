Full-day trip south of Quito to one of the world's highest active volcanoes — páramo landscapes, wild horses, condors, and glacier views.
<!--more-->

## Overview

Cotopaxi (5,897 m / 19,347 ft) dominates the southern Andes. This is a landscape day: hike around Limpiopungo Lake, ride horses across the páramo, trek toward José Rivas Refuge (~4,800 m), or bike down the slopes — then return to El Quinche by evening.

Much bigger “wow” factor than historic churches, if you prefer open terrain.

## Book ahead

Guided excursions (hiking, biking, horseback) should be booked in advance. The national park requires entrance registration.
