### (1.5–2 hrs south)
- One of the world’s highest active volcanoes (5,897 m).
- You can hike around Limpiopungo Lake, ride horses, or trek up to the mountain refuge (~4,800 m).
- Stunning páramo landscapes, wild horses, and condors.

### Overview
- Full-day trip **south of Quito (1.5–2 hrs)**.
- Cotopaxi (5,897 m / 19,347 ft) → one of the world’s highest active volcanoes.
- Activities: **[[Limpiopungo Lagoon]] hike, horseback riding, bike descent, or trek to José Rivas Refuge (~4,800 m / 15,800 ft)**.
- Landscapes: **Andean páramo, wild horses, condors, and glaciers looming above.**




- **Early AM departure (~6:30 AM)** → ~2 hrs south.
- Morning: **Limpiopungo Lagoon + hike to José Rivas Refuge** (if altitude allows).
- **Lunch** → Tambopaxi Lodge (_locro de papa, trout, hot chocolate_).
- Optional: guided mountain bike descent.
- Evening return → light dinner in El Quinche.
- Full-day trip **south to Cotopaxi National Park** (1.5–2 hrs).
- Morning: Hike to **José Rivas Refuge** (~15,800 ft) OR bike ride down volcano slopes.
- Lunch: Local hacienda (Hacienda Los Mortiños or Tambopaxi Lodge).
- Afternoon: Visit Limpiopungo Lagoon, wild horses, Andean scenery.
- Evening: Back to El Quinche.

    - _Trucha a la plancha_ (fresh trout).
