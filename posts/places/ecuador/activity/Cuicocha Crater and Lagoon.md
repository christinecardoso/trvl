## Schedule
A stunning volcanic crater lake with two islands in the middle, perfect for a short boat ride or hike around the rim.  

- Surrounding highland villages (Peguche, Cotacachi) known for waterfalls, music, and leather crafts. 

- Options:
	- **Short hike** along the rim (1–1.5 hrs, panoramic views).
	- **Boat ride** across the lagoon, with views of volcanic cliffs and surrounding Andean peaks.
- _Tip:_ Pack snacks from Otavalo (fruit, roasted corn, cheese) for this stretch.
