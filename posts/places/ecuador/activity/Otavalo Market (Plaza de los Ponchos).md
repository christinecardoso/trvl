(2 hrs north)
- Famous indigenous craft market (Saturday is the biggest).
- Known for textiles, alpaca sweaters, and jewelry.
- Nearby: **Cuicocha Lake**, a volcanic crater lagoon great for boat rides and short hikes.

<!--more-->

Highlights:  
- Vibrant stalls full of **textiles, alpaca sweaters, ponchos, rugs, and jewelry**.  
- Chance to meet local Kichwa artisans and enjoy authentic Andean culture.  
- Street food and local specialties available around the plaza.  

Nearby:  
- **Cuicocha Lake**, a dramatic volcanic crater lagoon, perfect for a short boat ride or hike around the rim.  
- Surrounding highland villages (Peguche, Cotacachi) known for waterfalls, music, and leather crafts. 

## Schedule
- Drive ~2 hrs north to **Otavalo**, home to South America’s most famous indigenous market.
- Even outside Saturday market day, stalls buzz with textiles, ponchos, weavings, leatherwork, jewelry, and handicrafts.
    
- **Snack while exploring:** grab **choclo con queso** (giant corn with cheese) or roasted nuts from vendors.

Have lunch in Otavalo
- **Mercado 24 de Mayo** – lively food hall with classics: **fritada** (crispy pork), **yahuarlocro** (hearty blood-sausage soup), and fresh juices.
    
- Alternatives:
    - **La Joya** – well-reviewed for traditional Ecuadorian dishes.  
    - **Shanandoa Coffee Lounge** – lighter café option, traveler-friendly, with WiFi.
