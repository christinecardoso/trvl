Famous indigenous craft market ~2 hrs north of Quito — textiles, alpaca sweaters, jewelry. Pair with Cuicocha lagoon or Peguche falls.
<!--more-->

## Overview

Plaza de los Ponchos is the heart of Otavalo’s artisan trade. Even outside Saturday market day, stalls sell weavings, leatherwork, and jewelry. Nearby highland villages add waterfalls, crater-lake views, and woodcarving.

## Tips

- Carry cash for market stalls and small cafés
- Pack a snack for the afternoon lagoon or waterfall stop
