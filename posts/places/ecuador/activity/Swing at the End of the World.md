Located at **Casa del Árbol**, this mountaintop swing sends you soaring over the valley with panoramic views of Tungurahua volcano.  

- A must-do photo stop near Baños.  
- Accessible by taxi, bus, or bike from town.  
- The site also has a small cafe and trails around the lookout.  

Best for: iconic photos, quick thrills, and scenic mountain views.
