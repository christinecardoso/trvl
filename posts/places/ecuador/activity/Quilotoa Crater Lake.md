### (3–3.5 hrs Drive)
- A turquoise volcanic lake inside a collapsed caldera.
- You can hike down to the water (~45 min) or walk part of the famous _Quilotoa Loop_ (multi-day trek).
- Local artisan villages on the way make it a cultural + scenic road trip.

<!--more-->
- **Scenic Wonder:** A breathtaking turquoise lake inside a collapsed volcanic caldera, with dramatic cliffs and panoramic Andean views.  
- **Hiking Options:** Steep 45–60 min hike down to the lake (allow double to climb back up). Mule rentals available for return. Full *Quilotoa Loop* (multi-day trek) passes through here. Rim trail (~5 hrs) for a challenging day hike with uninterrupted views.  
- **On the Lake:** Kayaks available for rent; unique way to experience the caldera from the water.  
- **Shopping & Culture:** Nearby artisan villages such as **Tigua** are famous for naïve-style paintings, textiles, and woodcarvings. Quilotoa’s small handicraft stalls sell ponchos, scarves, and souvenirs.  
- **Logistics:** The lake sits at ~3,914m (12,841 ft). Bring warm layers, water, and sun protection. Weather can shift quickly.  
- **Tips:** Visit in the morning for fewer clouds; afternoons often see mist rolling in. Roads are paved most of the way but can be winding. Private driver, tour, or rental car recommended for day-trippers from Quito.
