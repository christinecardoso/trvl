## Schedule
Indigenous market, Cuicocha crater lagoon, Peguche waterfall
- **Breakfast** → Cayambe stop for bizcochos + coffee.
- **Morning** → Explore [[Otavalo Market]].
- **Afternoon** → Visit [[Peguche Waterfall]] + [[Cuicocha crater]] lagoon.
- **Lunch** → Mercado 24 de Mayo (_fritada, yahuarlocro_).
- Optional: San Antonio de Ibarra (woodcarving town).
- Evening return.

- Visit **Peguche Waterfall** (sacred to locals).
- Stop at **Cuicocha Lagoon** for short hike or boat ride inside volcanic crater lake.
- Short hike Peguche Waterfall
- Optional detour to **San Antonio de Ibarra** (famous woodcarving town).
- Return to El Quinche in evening.
#### Food
- - **Breakfast stop (Cayambe again)**
    - _Bizcochos + coffee_ → at **Café Vaca Loca** (locals’ favorite).
- **Breakfast (early departure)** – Grab coffee & bizcochos in Cayambe again.
    - Drive 2 hrs north to **Otavalo Market** (even if not Saturday, still lively).
-  **Lunch (Otavalo)**
    - **Mercado 24 de Mayo** (main food market) → _fritada_, _yahuarlocro_, juices.
    - **Shanandoa Coffee Lounge** → lighter café meal (good if you need WiFi or a break).
- **Lunch (Otavalo)** –
    - _La Joya_ (well-reviewed for traditional dishes).
    - Or _Shanandoa Coffee Lounge_ (lighter, traveler-friendly café).
-  **Afternoon Snack**
    - Market stalls → _choclo con queso_ (giant corn + fresh cheese).
- **Afternoon (Cuicocha Lagoon)** – Pack snacks from Otavalo market (roasted corn, fruit, cheese).
- **Dinner (Return trip)** – If time, stop at **Hacienda Zuleta** (must reserve) for house-made cheeses, fresh farm produce, and condor sanctuary visit.
