## Schedule
**Breakfast Stop – Cayambe**

- Early departure north.
    
- Pause in **Cayambe** for the classic combo: **bizcochos** (buttery breadsticks), **queso de hoja** (leaf-wrapped cheese), and coffee.
    
- Local favorite: **Café Vaca Loca**.
    

---

**Morning – Otavalo Indigenous Market (2 hrs)**

- Drive ~2 hrs north to **Otavalo**, home to South America’s most famous indigenous market.
    
- Even outside Saturday market day, stalls buzz with textiles, ponchos, weavings, leatherwork, jewelry, and handicrafts.
    
- **Snack while exploring:** grab **choclo con queso** (giant corn with cheese) or roasted nuts from vendors.
    

---

**Lunch in Otavalo (1 hr)**

- **Mercado 24 de Mayo** – lively food hall with classics: **fritada** (crispy pork), **yahuarlocro** (hearty blood-sausage soup), and fresh juices.
    
- Alternatives:
    
    - **La Joya** – well-reviewed for traditional Ecuadorian dishes.
        
    - **Shanandoa Coffee Lounge** – lighter café option, traveler-friendly, with WiFi.
        

---

**Afternoon – Peguche Waterfall & Cuicocha Crater Lagoon (3 hrs)**

- **Peguche Waterfall** – sacred site for the local Kichwa community. Short, easy trail (15–20 min walk) leads through eucalyptus groves to the falls. Often used for traditional bathing ceremonies.
    
- Continue to **Cuicocha Lagoon**, a stunning volcanic crater lake with two islands in the middle.
    
    - Options:
        
        - **Short hike** along the rim (1–1.5 hrs, panoramic views).
            
        - **Boat ride** across the lagoon, with views of volcanic cliffs and surrounding Andean peaks.
            
- _Tip:_ Pack snacks from Otavalo (fruit, roasted corn, cheese) for this stretch.
    
---

**Optional Detour – San Antonio de Ibarra**

- If time allows, stop in **San Antonio de Ibarra**, a town world-renowned for its **woodcarving artisans**. Workshops welcome visitors to see traditional craft.
    

---

**Dinner / Return**

- Evening drive south toward Quito/El Quinche (~2 hrs).
    
- If time and planning allow, make a special stop at **Hacienda Zuleta** (advance reservation required):
    
    - Farm-to-table dinner with house-made cheeses and organic produce.
        
    - Visit the **Condor Huasi Project** (condor sanctuary).
        
- Otherwise, enjoy a light dinner en route back.
