## Schedule
- Just behind the Termas lodge is the **Cayambe-Coca Ecological Reserve**, a protected high-altitude cloud forest.
    
- Well-marked trails (20–45 min loops) take you through **misty páramo landscapes, native orchids, moss-covered trees, and crystal-clear streams**.
    
- Highlights:
    
    - **Waterfall Trail** – easy 30–40 min loop to a series of cascading falls.
        
    - Birdlife: keep an eye out for hummingbirds, tanagers, and sometimes Andean guans.
        
    - Breathtaking views of **Papallacta River valley** framed by cloud-kissed peaks.
        
- Perfect for stretching your legs before dinner, and trails start right at the lodge entrance.
