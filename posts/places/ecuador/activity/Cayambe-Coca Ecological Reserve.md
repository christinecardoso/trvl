## Schedule

**Short Hike into Cayambe-Coca Reserve**, a protected high-altitude cloud forest. → Trails begin just behind the lodge. Choose the **Waterfall Trail** (30–40 min loop) or a longer path (up to 1 hr) through cloud forest and páramo terrain. Expect **misty páramo landscapes, native orchids, moss-draped trees, crystal-clear streams, and birdlife** (hummingbirds, tanagers). The route opens up to **valley viewpoints** and ends back at the lodge
    
- Highlights:
    - **Waterfall Trail** – easy 30–40 min loop to a series of cascading falls.
    - Birdlife: keep an eye out for hummingbirds, tanagers, and sometimes Andean guans.
    - Breathtaking views of **Papallacta River valley** framed by cloud-kissed peaks.
- Perfect for stretching your legs before dinner, and trails start right at the lodge entrance.
