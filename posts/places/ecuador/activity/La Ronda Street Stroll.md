## Schedule

Stroll this **colonial pedestrian street** famed for lantern-lit balconies, artisan workshops, and sweet shops. 
Stop at:
- **Dulcería Colonial**
- browse **La Casona de las Artesanías**
- Sip at **Café La Ronda**.
