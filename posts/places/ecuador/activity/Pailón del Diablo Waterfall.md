The most famous stop on the **Ruta de las Cascadas**, this roaring waterfall drops through a gorge surrounded by lush jungle.  

- Short hike (20–30 min) leads to multiple viewpoints.  
- You can climb behind the waterfall for a misty adventure.  
- Swinging suspension bridge adds to the thrill.  

Best for: nature lovers, photographers, and anyone wanting a memorable Baños waterfall hike.
