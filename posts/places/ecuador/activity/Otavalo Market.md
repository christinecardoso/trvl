(2 hrs north)
- Famous indigenous craft market (Saturday is the biggest).
- Known for textiles, alpaca sweaters, and jewelry.
- Nearby: **Cuicocha Lake**, a volcanic crater lagoon great for boat rides and short hikes.

<!--more-->

Highlights:  
- Vibrant stalls full of **textiles, alpaca sweaters, ponchos, rugs, and jewelry**.  
- Chance to meet local Kichwa artisans and enjoy authentic Andean culture.  
- Street food and local specialties available around the plaza.  

Nearby:  
- **Cuicocha Lake**, a dramatic volcanic crater lagoon, perfect for a short boat ride or hike around the rim.  
- Surrounding highland villages (Peguche, Cotacachi) known for waterfalls, music, and leather crafts.
