## Schedule
Cable car up Pichincha Volcano; great for hikes (book in good weather).

Ride up to ~13,000 ft for valley-and-volcano views. If skies are clear, sample the easy stretch of the **[[Rucu Pichincha]]** trail (~1 hr). Go slow—altitude is real.

*Tips:*
- *Bring layers; it’s cooler at altitude on TelefériQo.*
- *If skies are hazy, skip the hike and linger longer in Centro Histórico.*
