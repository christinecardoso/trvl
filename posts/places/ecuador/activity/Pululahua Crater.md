## Overview

Pululahua is a geobotanical reserve just north of Quito. Most visitors stop at the rim mirador; longer trips hike into the valley or ride horses on the floor. Pair with [[Mitad del Mundo]] and [[Intiñan Solar Museum]].

## Tips

- Go early before clouds fill the crater
- Bring layers — cooler and damper than Quito
- Rim viewpoints are enough if you skip the descent
