## 🌮 [[places/essentials/Things You Have to Eat]]

- **Locro de Papa** – A rich potato and cheese soup, often topped with avocado. Quito’s comfort food.
- **Llapingachos** – Potato patties stuffed with cheese, served with peanut sauce.
- **Hornado** – Slow-roasted pork, usually found in markets (Mercado Central, Mercado Iñaquito).
- **Ceviche Quiteño** – Made with shrimp or chochos (Andean lupini beans), served with popcorn and plantain chips.
- **Empanadas de Viento** – Airy, fried empanadas filled with cheese and dusted with sugar.
- **Fresh Fruit & Juices** – Naranjilla, babaco, tree tomato, guanábana—try juices at local markets.
- **Canelazo** – A hot spiced cinnamon drink with aguardiente, perfect for chilly Quito nights.
    

👉 _Tip_: Food tours in Quito’s Centro Histórico or at La Floresta neighborhood are worth booking ahead for a guided taste experience.

## 🏞️ Types of Places to Visit

- **Centro Histórico (UNESCO World Heritage Site)** – Quito’s old town with plazas, colonial churches, and colorful streets.
- **Basilica del Voto Nacional** – Climb the towers for panoramic views.
- **El Panecillo & Virgen of Quito** – Hilltop statue overlooking the city.
- **La Ronda Street** – Evening stroll with traditional sweets, crafts, and music.
- **Mercados** – Mercado Central or Iñaquito for local food and daily life.
- **La Floresta** – Bohemian neighborhood with street art, cafes, and indie vibes.
- **TelefériQo** – Cable car up Pichincha Volcano; great for hikes (book in good weather).

## 🎟️ Unique Experiences & Advance Bookings

- **Mitad del Mundo** – Stand on the equator line; nearby Intiñan Museum is more scientific and interactive.
- **Day Trip to Otavalo Market** – Famous indigenous craft market (best on Saturdays).
- **Cotopaxi Volcano Excursion** – Needs a guide; book ahead if you want to hike or bike.
- **Papallacta Hot Springs** – Scenic drive and natural thermal pools in the mountains.
- **Mindo Cloud Forest** – Reserve ziplining, chocolate tours, or birdwatching in advance.
- **Mashpi Lodge (Luxury Cloud Forest Stay)** – Needs reservations far in advance; highly exclusive.

## 🌟 Unique to the Region

- **Altitude Experience** – Quito sits at ~9,350 ft (2,850 m). Take it slow first day.
- **Chiva Party Bus** – Colorful open-air bus with music and dancing, a fun nightlife twist.
- **Traditional Herbal Healing (Curandería)** – Can be arranged in local markets for a cultural experience.
- **Andean Condor Watching** – Guided trips from Quito give you the chance to spot Ecuador’s national bird.

## 🌋 Nature & Adventure Near Quito

- **[[Cotopaxi National Park]]** (1.5–2 hrs south)
	- One of the world’s highest active volcanoes (5,897 m).
	- You can hike around Limpiopungo Lake, ride horses, or trek up to the mountain refuge (~4,800 m).
	- Stunning páramo landscapes, wild horses, and condors.
- **[[Quilotoa Crater Lake]]** (3–3.5 hrs)
	- A turquoise volcanic lake inside a collapsed caldera.
	- You can hike down to the water (~45 min) or walk part of the famous _Quilotoa Loop_ (multi-day trek).
	- Local artisan villages on the way make it a cultural + scenic road trip.
- **[[places/ecuador/activity/Mindo Cloud Forest]]** (2 hrs northwest)
	- A lush cloud forest full of birds, waterfalls, butterflies, and orchids.
	- Popular for chocolate tours, coffee farms, tubing down rivers, and zip-lining.
	- A good overnight trip if you love eco-lodges and nature sounds.
- **[[Papallacta Hot Springs]]** (1.5 hrs east)
	- Natural thermal pools surrounded by Andean mountains.
	- Perfect after hiking or for acclimating to altitude.
	- You can combine with nearby páramo hikes or waterfalls.

## 🏞 Cultural & Scenic Villages

### **[[places/ecuador/activity/Otavalo Market (Plaza de los Ponchos)]]** (2 hrs north)

- Famous indigenous craft market (Saturday is the biggest).
- Known for textiles, alpaca sweaters, and jewelry.
- Nearby: **Cuicocha Lake**, a volcanic crater lagoon great for boat rides and short hikes.

### **[[Baños de Agua Santa]]** (3.5 hrs south)

- Adventure capital of Ecuador.
- Activities: waterfalls route, swing at the end of the world, canyoning, biking, hot springs.
- Good if you want a mix of adrenaline and lush scenery.

---

## 🌿 Unique Add-Ons

- **[[places/ecuador/activity/Pululahua Crater]]** (30 min from Quito) – an inhabited volcanic crater with hiking trails and viewpoints.
- **[[Antisana Ecological Reserve]]** (2 hrs) – less touristy than Cotopaxi, with amazing wildlife (spectacled bears, condors).
- **[[Mashpi Lodge]]** (3 hrs) – ultra-luxury eco-lodge in pristine cloud forest.

- **5+ days:** Include Baños or even continue on to the Amazon (Tena, Coca) or Galápagos (by flight).

## ✈️ Tips for August

- It’s the **dry season** in the Andes → best time for hiking and clear views.
- Evenings/nights are chilly (~50°F / 10°C), so pack layers.
- Altitude in Quito is high (9,350 ft / 2,850 m), so take it easy the first day.
