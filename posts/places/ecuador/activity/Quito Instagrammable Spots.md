Photo-forward Quito stops — pair with TelefériQo, Centro Histórico, and Panecillo for clear-sky shots.

- **TelefériQo / Cruz Loma** — city + volcano backdrop
- **Plaza Grande & Compañía façade** — golden hour in Centro Histórico
- **El Panecillo** — classic Virgen overlook
- **Guápulo church overlook** — valley light late afternoon
- **Parque Itchimbía** — skyline without the full Panecillo climb
