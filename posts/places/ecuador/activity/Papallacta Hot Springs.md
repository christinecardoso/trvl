## Overview

Papallacta sits ~1.5 hrs east of Quito in Ecuador’s high páramo. Trails start at the lodge entrance; thermal pools and spa treatments are the main draw. Weather flips fast — layers and rain gear matter.

## Book ahead

- Spa treatments or private pools at Termas de Papallacta
- Overnight at [[Mamallacta Paramo Lodge]] if you want after-dark soaking

## Hiking tips

- Altitude is real (3,300–4,000 m) — go slow
- Mornings are clearest; afternoons cloud over
- Trails can be muddy — waterproof shoes help
