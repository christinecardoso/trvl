Where to stay for one night: 
[[Mamallacta Paramo Lodge]]

 If you’re basing yourself at **Termas Papallacta / Cayambe-Coca entrance**, you’re literally sitting in the middle of Ecuador’s **páramo** ecosystem (high-altitude moorlands, ~3,300–4,000 m). Here are the main nearby **páramo hikes** (all within 10–30 min of Papallacta):
## 🌿 **Right Behind Termas Papallacta**

- **Waterfall Trail Loops**
    
    - **Time:** 20–45 min loops.
        
    - **What you see:** Misty páramo streams, orchids, mossy cloud forest patches.
        
    - **Difficulty:** Easy. Great warm-up at altitude.
        
    - **Trailhead:** Directly behind the lodge (–0.3695, –78.1432).
        

---

## 🏔 **La Virgen – Papallacta Trail**

- **Time:** 4–5 hrs one-way (can turn back at any point).
    
- **What you see:** Expansive páramo landscapes, views down into Papallacta valley, Andean flora like chuquiragua.
    
- **Notes:** This is a section of the historic **Quito–Baeza mule track**.
    
- **Trailhead:** At the **La Virgen pass** (–0.349, –78.150), a 10 min drive uphill from Papallacta.
    

---

## 🌋 **Laguna Parcacocha (Papallacta Lakes)**

- **Time:** 2–3 hrs out & back.
    
- **What you see:** High-altitude glacial lake framed by páramo peaks; excellent birdwatching (Andean gulls, ducks).
    
- **Trailhead:** Dirt road north of Papallacta spa leads to the **Laguna Parcacocha** area (–0.357, –78.140).
    

---

## 🌄 **Antisana Buffer Zone (short hikes from Papallacta Road)**

- **Antenas Viewpoint Hike**
    
    - **Time:** 1–2 hrs roundtrip.
        
    - **What you see:** Sweeping views toward Antisana volcano (if clear), plus páramo plains.
        
    - **Trailhead:** About 15 min from Papallacta along the road toward Píntag.
        

---

## 🦅 **Laguna de Papallacta (above the town)**

- **Time:** 1–1.5 hrs circuit around the lake.
    
- **What you see:** Wind-swept páramo, waterfowl, surrounding ridges.
    
- **Trailhead:** Access road north of Papallacta village (–0.371, –78.126).
    

---

✅ **Tips for hiking in páramo near Papallacta**:

- Go **slow** — the trails sit between 3,300–4,000 m; altitude is real.
    
- Weather changes in minutes: bring **layers + rain gear**.
    
- Trails can be **muddy** — waterproof shoes are best.
    
- Morning is clearest; afternoons cloud over quickly.
    

## Schedule
- Spend the afternoon soaking in the **thermal pools**, enjoying **spa treatments**, and relaxing with mountain views.

If you Stay at Termas de Papallacta lodge, you can get cozy overnight surrounded by mountains. Guests have **after-dark pool access** for a magical soak under the stars.
