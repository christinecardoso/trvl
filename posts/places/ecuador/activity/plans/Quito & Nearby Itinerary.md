# Quito & Nearby Itinerary (Landing 7 PM Tuesday)

## **Day 1: Tuesday – Arrival**

- **7 PM Arrival at Quito Airport (UIO)** → 45–60 min drive into the city.
- **Check-in** at hotel/Airbnb.
- Light dinner/snack near lodging if hungry (soups like **locro de papa** or **caldo de gallina** are comforting).
- Rest — important for altitude adjustment (~9,350 ft).

👉 _Don’t plan sightseeing tonight — focus on settling in._

---

## **Day 2: Wednesday – Historic Quito + TelefériQo**

- **Morning** – Explore **Centro Histórico** (Plaza Grande, San Francisco, La Compañía de Jesús).
- **Midday** – Lunch at Mercado Central (try hornado or fried corvina + fruit juice).
- **Afternoon** – **TelefériQo cable car** for city and volcano views. (Go early if skies are clear, switch timing if cloudy.)
- **Evening** – Dinner at **Urko** (book ahead) or La Floresta neighborhood.

---

## **Day 3: Thursday – Equator & Pululahua**

- **Morning** – **Mitad del Mundo monument** + **Intiñan Museum** (science/cultural exhibits).
- **Lunch stop** – Cayambe for **bizcochos with cheese and hot chocolate**.
- **Afternoon** – Short visit to **Pululahua crater reserve** nearby.
- **Back by 3–4 PM** → wedding events start.

---

## **Day 4–5: Friday & Saturday – Wedding Days**

- Keep energy for events.
    
- If free in the **mornings** → short stroll in Parque La Carolina or Mercado Iñaquito breakfast (empanadas, jugo de naranjilla).
    

---

## **Day 6: Sunday – Recovery & Hot Springs**

- **Morning** – Wedding wrap-up.
- **Afternoon** – Drive to **Papallacta Hot Springs** (1.5 hrs).
- Overnight stay at Termas de Papallacta resort.
- Soak, spa, short nature walks in Cayambe-Coca.

---

## **Day 7: Monday – Cloud Forest or Market**

Choose your vibe:

- **Mindo (2 hrs NW)** – Adventure/nature. Birdwatching, waterfalls, chocolate, ziplines.
- **Otavalo (2 hrs N)** – Culture/shopping. Textiles, Cuicocha Lagoon, Peguche Waterfall.

Both can be day trips (leave ~7 AM, back ~7 PM).

---

## **Day 8: Tuesday – Departure**

- If **AM flight** → Just transfer to airport.
- If **PM flight** → Brunch at **Casa Gangotena** in old town, last stroll through La Ronda for sweets/souvenirs, then head to airport (leave ~4 hrs before flight — Quito airport is far).

---

✅ With the **7 PM Tuesday landing**, the flow becomes:

- **Wed/Thu pre-wedding** → Quito highlights + Mitad del Mundo.
    
- **Fri–Sat** → Wedding focus.
    
- **Sun–Tue post-wedding** → Papallacta + one big day trip (Mindo or Otavalo).
