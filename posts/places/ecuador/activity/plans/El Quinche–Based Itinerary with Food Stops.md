## **Day 1: Tuesday – Arrival**

- **Arrival 7 PM** → Transfer 25 min to El Quinche.
- **Dinner Option (El Quinche)**
    - **Caldo de Gallina stall (chicken soup)** near the Basilica. Simple but hearty.        
    - Or light empanadas from the plaza vendors.
- Rest, hydrate for altitude.

- **Dinner** → _Plaza de El Quinche_, Basilica plaza food stalls:
	-  Street stalls in front of the **Basilica of El Quinche** → _caldo de gallina_, _empanadas de viento_, fresh juices.
    - Simple, local, and authentic.
    - _Caldo de gallina_ (chicken soup with a whole leg).
    - _Empanadas de viento_ (cheese, fried, dusted with sugar).
    - Fresh jugo (naranjilla or mora).

## **Day 2: Wednesday – Quito Historic Center**

- **Breakfast (en route)** – Fresh juice & empanada stop in El Quinche market.
- **Morning–Afternoon** → Drive into **Centro Histórico** (1 hr).    
    - Plaza Grande, San Francisco Church, La Compañía de Jesús (gold interior).
    - Basilica del Voto Nacional towers - Climb towers of **Basilica del Voto Nacional** for views
    - Lunch at Mercado Central (hornado, locro de papa).
- **Lunch (Centro Histórico)**
    - **Mercado Central** – Try _hornado_ (slow-roasted pork with mote) and jugo de mora.
    - Or **San Ignacio Cafetería** (great for coffee + sweets).
- **Afternoon** – **TelefériQo cable car**. If skies are clear, take a short hike on Rucu Pichincha trail (easy stretch only).
- **Late Afternoon** – La Ronda street for coffee & sweets.
- **Dinner (Back toward El Quinche)**
    - Stop in **La Floresta** before returning:
        - **Urko** (modern tasting menu, reserve ahead).
        - Or tasting menu at **Ciré** (rooftop dining, Andean fusion).        
- **Evening** → Return to El Quinche (avoid late-night Quito traffic).

- **Late Morning** – Continue north to **Cayambe** for bizcochos, queso de hoja, hot chocolate.
- **Afternoon** – Detour stop at **Pululahua crater reserve** (people live inside a volcanic crater!).
- **Evening** – Back to El Quinche to rest before wedding weekend.

- **Breakfast** – At Airbnb or light fruit from El Quinche market.
- **Midday Snack (Mitad del Mundo)** – Street stalls for _helados de paila_ (hand-stirred ice cream).
- **Lunch (Cayambe)**
    - Famous **bizcochos + queso de hoja + hot chocolate** at _Bizcochos San Pedro_ or _Bizcochos Don Pedro_.
    - Try local _queso de hoja_ (fresh cheese wrapped in leaves). 
- **Afternoon** – Pululahua crater viewpoint → bring along bizcochos & cheese as snack.
- **Dinner** – Back in El Quinche, low-key evening (maybe try _tripas mishqui_ from plaza stalls if adventurous).

- **Breakfast (El Quinche)** → _Humitas_ (steamed corn cakes with cheese).
- **Midday Snack (Mitad del Mundo)** → _Helados de paila_ (fruit sorbet churned in copper pans).

    - Famous _bizcochos_ (buttery breadsticks).

- **Afternoon** → Pululahua crater picnic with leftover bizcochos + cheese.

## **Day 4: Friday – [[Local + Welcome Dinner]]**

- Explore **El Quinche** itself:
    - Basilica of El Quinche (pilgrimage site, worth seeing inside).
    - Small local market for fruit and street food.
- Keep day light → **Welcome Dinner** in the evening.

- **Breakfast** – Fresh _humitas_ (steamed corn cakes) from El Quinche market.
- **Lunch** – Small almuerzo (set lunch) near Basilica (options change daily).
- **Evening** – Wedding welcome dinner.

## **Day 6: Sunday – Recovery in Papallacta**

- **Morning** – Likely wedding brunch/farewells.
- **Afternoon (en route to Papallacta)** – Short drive (1 hr east) to **Papallacta Hot Springs**. 
- **Lunch** - Stop at roadside trout farms (_trucha a la plancha_ or _trucha al ajillo_). Super fresh and traditional.
- - **Lunch (on way)** – Trout farms just before Papallacta: _trucha al ajillo_ (garlic trout) or _trucha a la plancha_.
	-  **La Trucha Dorada** (highway trout farm before Papallacta) → _trucha al ajillo_ (garlic trout).
- **Afternoon** – Papallacta Hot Springs (soak, spa).
- Check in at Termas de Papallacta →  Soak, spa, relax. Thermal pools, spa treatments, mountain views.
- **Late Afternoon** – Short hike into Cayambe-Coca reserve for waterfalls.
- **Dinner** – **Termas de Papallacta lodge restaurant** – warming soups (locro, quinoa soup) and river trout.
- **Dinner (Termas restaurant)** – _Quinoa soup_, fresh trout, or Andean lamb.
- Overnight stay.
- **Lunch (en route)**

## **Day 7: Monday – Day Trip Adventure**

### Otavalo & Cuicocha
- Visit **Peguche Waterfall** (sacred to locals).
- Stop at **Cuicocha Lagoon** for short hike or boat ride inside volcanic crater lake.
- Short hike Peguche Waterfall
- Optional detour to **San Antonio de Ibarra** (famous woodcarving town).
- Return to El Quinche in evening.
#### Food
- - **Breakfast stop (Cayambe again)**
    - _Bizcochos + coffee_ → at **Café Vaca Loca** (locals’ favorite).
- **Breakfast (early departure)** – Grab coffee & bizcochos in Cayambe again.
    - Drive 2 hrs north to **Otavalo Market** (even if not Saturday, still lively).
-  **Lunch (Otavalo)**
    - **Mercado 24 de Mayo** (main food market) → _fritada_, _yahuarlocro_, juices.
    - **Shanandoa Coffee Lounge** → lighter café meal (good if you need WiFi or a break).
- **Lunch (Otavalo)** –
    - _La Joya_ (well-reviewed for traditional dishes).
    - Or _Shanandoa Coffee Lounge_ (lighter, traveler-friendly café).
-  **Afternoon Snack**
    - Market stalls → _choclo con queso_ (giant corn + fresh cheese).
- **Afternoon (Cuicocha Lagoon)** – Pack snacks from Otavalo market (roasted corn, fruit, cheese).
- **Dinner (Return trip)** – If time, stop at **Hacienda Zuleta** (must reserve) for house-made cheeses, fresh farm produce, and condor sanctuary visit.

### Other Options
Options from El Quinche are easier than from Quito, Choose depending on energy:
- **Otavalo (2 hrs north)** – Indigenous market, Cuicocha crater lagoon, Peguche waterfall.
- **[[Mindo Cloud Forest]] (3 hrs NW)** – More driving from El Quinche, but possible if you leave early. Best for zipline, chocolate tours, birdwatching.
- **Hacienda Zuleta (2.5 hrs)** – Historic hacienda stay with condor rehab, cheese-making, horseback riding.

## **Day 7: Monday C – Cotopaxi Volcano Adventure**

- Full-day trip **south to Cotopaxi National Park** (1.5–2 hrs).
- Morning: Hike to **José Rivas Refuge** (~15,800 ft) OR bike ride down volcano slopes.
- Lunch: Local hacienda (Hacienda Los Mortiños or Tambopaxi Lodge).
- Afternoon: Visit Limpiopungo Lagoon, wild horses, Andean scenery.
- Evening: Back to El Quinche.

👉 Much bigger “wow” factor than historic churches, if you prefer landscapes.

- **Breakfast (roadside stop)** → _Tigrillo_ (green plantain mashed with cheese & eggs) + coffee.
- **Lunch (hacienda lodge in park)** →
    - _Trucha a la plancha_ (fresh trout).
    - _Locro de papa_ (potato-cheese soup with avocado).
- **Snack** → Hot chocolate + _queso fresco_ at Tambopaxi Lodge, overlooking volcano.

- **Breakfast (roadside stop, Sangolquí/Pintag route)**
    - _Café de la Vaca_ (Machachi area) → _tigrillo_ (plantain mash) + coffee.
- **Lunch (inside Cotopaxi)**
    - **Tambopaxi Lodge** (right in the park) → _locro de papa_, hot chocolate + _queso fresco_, trout with volcano views.
- **Optional Snack** → _Hacienda Los Mortiños_ (scenic spot just outside park).

# 🌟 Must-Try Foods by Region

- **Quito:** Locro de papa, empanadas de viento, canelazo (spiced aguardiente drink).
- **Cayambe:** Bizcochos, queso de hoja, hot chocolate.
- **Papallacta:** Fresh trout, quinoa soup.
- **Otavalo:** Indigenous stews, mote (hominy), traditional sweets.
- **On the road:** Helados de paila (fruit ice cream), street roasted choclo con queso.

---

# ✅ Why This Works from El Quinche

- **City gets one focused day** (Wednesday) so you don’t keep commuting in/out.
- **Thursday = northbound** (Equator, Cayambe, Pululahua).
- **Post-wedding = east/north** (Papallacta + Otavalo/Cuicocha), much easier from your base.
- You still get a mix of history, culture, and nature without exhausting travel.

# ✅ Key Adjustments for El Quinche

- **Proximity advantage**: You’re closer to airport, Papallacta, Cayambe, Otavalo.
- **Trade-off**: You’ll spend more time commuting into Quito Centro Histórico (but just one dedicated day is enough).
- **Unique local flavor**: The Basilica of El Quinche is a destination in itself. Staying there gives you a more authentic village feel versus urban Quito.

✅ This way, every big outing doubles as a **culinary adventure** — you’ll hit the classics (Quito street food + markets) and the unique-to-the-region foods (bizcochos in Cayambe, trout in Papallacta, indigenous stews in Otavalo).
