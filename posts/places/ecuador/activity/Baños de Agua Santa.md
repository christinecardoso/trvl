(3.5 hrs south)

Known as the **adventure capital of Ecuador**, Baños is nestled between lush mountains and waterfalls, offering a blend of adrenaline and relaxation.  

Highlights:  
- **Ruta de las Cascadas** (Waterfall Route): bike or drive past a series of stunning waterfalls, including **Pailón del Diablo**.  
- **Swing at the End of the World**: iconic mountaintop swing with dramatic views.  
- **Canyoning** and **rafting** for thrill-seekers.  
- Natural **thermal hot springs** to relax after adventures.  
- A lively town center with cafes, restaurants, and artisan shops.  

Best for: travelers who want a mix of adventure sports, breathtaking landscapes, and downtime in hot springs.
