Best pick if you want **golf + Miami Beach** in one morning.

<!--more-->

Historic par-72 public course right off Alton Road — palms, water, and a surprisingly secluded feel considering you’re basically in South Beach.

### Saturday pattern

Tee off **~7:30–8:00 AM**, finish **~noon**, eat/drink at the clubhouse, then continue (Betsy / pool / boat). Club listed open **7 AM–8 PM** Saturday.

- **Area:** Miami Beach (Alton Road)
- **Vibe:** Historic Beach golf — location win
