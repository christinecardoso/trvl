### Set flavor

Includes **Clocks, Fix You, The Scientist, Viva la Vida, Radioactive,** and **Believer**.

### Why the venue matters

**Hotel Colonnade** has dramatic Coral Gables architecture — grand interiors, rotunda/dome, marble and old-world detailing — rather than a generic concert hall. That makes this more interesting than “pop covers in a black box.”

Romantic, intimate, visually beautiful. Easy to pair with a Coral Gables dinner (e.g. Armstrong) before or after.
