🌅 **Wynwood rooftop/pool scene** — ART Wynwood (Arlo Roof Top) is open from **noon** with the open-air rooftop/pool setup.

<!--more-->

More **daycation + music + cocktails** than a rave — choose it for atmosphere rather than a specific DJ lineup… unless you’re there for **STAMPED**.

### STAMPED (4–11 PM)

Afrobeats + amapiano + sunset. Afro B with AYA & Friends, FiiFii, Milli Marv on the 9th-floor ART Wynwood pool deck.

Compared with Novo Flow: **Novo = house + sax + dancers. STAMPED = Afrobeats/amapiano + sunset + Wynwood crowd.**

- **Where:** Arlo Wynwood — ART Wynwood pool deck
- **Area:** Wynwood
