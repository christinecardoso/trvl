If you're eyeing **Okeechobee Steakhouse** in West Palm Beach for a birthday celebration, you’re in for something truly special. Here's what makes it stand out:

---

### Birthday Perk: Free Steak Dinner

When you join their **VIP Rewards program**, you get a **free 8 oz Palm Beach Sirloin dinner on your actual birthday**—provided you purchase another entrée of equal or greater value. You'll need to **show valid ID** on-site, and it's not valid with other offers or for to-go orders.([Okeechobee Steakhouse](https://www.okeesteakhouse.com/vip-rewards?utm_source=chatgpt.com "VIP Rewards | Okeechobee Steakhouse - West Palm ..."))

Over the past 30 years, they’ve given away **350,000 free birthday dinners**, making this one of the most iconic birthday traditions in the area.([Spike on Golf & Travel](https://spikeongolfandtravel.com/happy-b-day-x-350000-at-okeechobee-steakhouse/?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Happy B-Day x 350000 at Okeechobee Steakhouse"))

### Decadent Dessert Offerings

To top off your steak with something sweet, Okeechobee offers a few tempting desserts:

- **Blueberry Dump** — baked blueberries under a sweet crust, served with homemade gelato.([Okeechobee Steak](https://okeechobee-steak.res-menu.com/menu?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Okeechobee Steak Menu (Full Menu Update 2025)"))
    
- **Cheesecake** — ask your server about the day’s flavor options.([Okeechobee Steak](https://okeechobee-steak.res-menu.com/menu?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Okeechobee Steak Menu (Full Menu Update 2025)"))
    
- **Chocolate Fudge Brownie Bites** — topped with their house-made gelato.([Okeechobee Steak](https://okeechobee-steak.res-menu.com/menu?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Okeechobee Steak Menu (Full Menu Update 2025)"))
    
- Reviewers rave about the cheesecake and the bread pudding, calling them “one of the best in recent years” and “on par with McCormick & Schmick’s,” respectively.([Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/menu/okeechobee-steak-house-west-palm-beach/item/cheesecake?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Cheesecake - Menu - Okeechobee Steak House"), [Check Please FL](https://www.checkpleasefl.com/restaurants/okeechobee-steakhouse/?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Okeechobee Steakhouse - Check, Please! - And for a visually dramatic finale, they offer a signature dessert called **The King’s Pie**—a peanut butter mousse encased in a chocolate shell, topped with meringue, and finished tableside with flamed banana liqueur.([Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/okeechobeesteakhouse/?hl=en&utm_source=chatgpt.com "Florida's Oldest Steakhouse (@okeechobeesteakhouse)"))
