## Independent Dessert Spots (Great for Birthday Treats)

These locally loved sweet spots bring creativity and flavor to birthday celebrations:

**Ganache Bakery**

- Owned by a Food Network star, this bakery is renowned for its **key lime tarts**, **rum cakes**, **carrot cake cupcakes**, and custom birthday cakes. A standout pick if you're after something both delicious and visually stunning.([West Palm Beach Food Tours](https://www.westpalmbeachfoodtour.com/blog/best-desserts-in-west-palm-beach/?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Best Desserts in West Palm Beach"), [Wanderlog](https://wanderlog.com/list/geoCategory/26903/best-desserts-and-snacks-in-west-palm-beach?utm_source=chatgpt.com "The 42 best desserts and snacks in West Palm Beach"))
    

**Biscuit Lady’s Bakehouse**

- A hidden gem tucked in a warehouse district just south of downtown. Known for over-the-top treats like half-pound stuffed cookies, banana pudding, cobblers, and gigantic cake slices—perfect for a decadent birthday indulgence.([West Palm Beach Food Tours](https://www.westpalmbeachfoodtour.com/blog/best-desserts-in-west-palm-beach/?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Best Desserts in West Palm Beach"))
    

**Hive Bakery & Café**

- Cute, Instagram-worthy pastries in a beautifully designed space. Favorites include a rich brownie and brown-butter cashew blondie—ideal for a cozy birthday afternoon.([West Palm Beach Food Tours](https://www.westpalmbeachfoodtour.com/blog/best-desserts-in-west-palm-beach/?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Best Desserts in West Palm Beach"), [Southern Living](https://www.southernliving.com/west-palm-beach-fl-8761803?utm_source=chatgpt.com "15 Best Things To Do In West Palm Beach, Florida"))
    

**Cholo Soy Cocina**

- Known for tacos, but don’t skip dessert: housemade **churros with dulce de leche** are reportedly outstanding.([West Palm Beach Food Tours](https://www.westpalmbeachfoodtour.com/blog/best-desserts-in-west-palm-beach/?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Best Desserts in West Palm Beach"))
    

**International Favorites**:

- **Loic Bakery & Café Bar**—famous for the best pistachio croissants (and more).([Wanderlog](https://wanderlog.com/list/geoCategory/26903/best-desserts-and-snacks-in-west-palm-beach?utm_source=chatgpt.com "The 42 best desserts and snacks in West Palm Beach"))
    
- **Earth & Sugar**—great for custom cakes or vegan options; praised for its creativity and service.([Wanderlog](https://wanderlog.com/list/geoCategory/26903/best-desserts-and-snacks-in-west-palm-beach?utm_source=chatgpt.com "The 42 best desserts and snacks in West Palm Beach"))
    
- **Sloan’s Ice Cream**—a fun, carnival-themed ice cream parlor with wacky creations like "Kitchen Sink" sundaes.([Wanderlog](https://wanderlog.com/list/geoCategory/26903/best-desserts-and-snacks-in-west-palm-beach?utm_source=chatgpt.com "The 42 best desserts and snacks in West Palm Beach"), [Tripadvisor](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurants-g12664693-zfg9909-The_Palm_Beaches_Florida.html?utm_source=chatgpt.com "THE BEST Dessert in The Palm Beaches"))
