## Discover Creative Adult Classes in South Florida

Looking for hands-on art experiences in South Florida? Whether you're craving the tactile thrill of jewelry making, the glow of glass art, or the versatility of painting and mixed media, these brilliant institutions have something for every creative soul.

---

## **1. Armory Art Center (West Palm Beach)**

_For the Mixed media explorer_

**Overview**  
The Armory Art Center offers nearly 100 diverse courses across 12 state-of-the-art studios, spanning ceramics, painting, jewelry, sculpture, photography, digital arts, and more ([Armory Art Center](https://armoryart.org/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)).

### 🎨 Painting

- _Crash Course in Painting (Oil & Acrylic)_ – beginner friendly.
    
- _Alla Prima Drawing & Painting_ – direct painting techniques.
    
- _Intro & Intermediate Oil Painting_.
    
- _Studio Landscape Painting_ – landscapes from photo references.
    
- _Intermediate Watercolor_.
    

### 💍 Jewelry & Metalsmithing

- _Intro to Jewelry and Metalsmithing_.
    
- _Intro to Enameling_.
    
- _Wire Work (beginner to intermediate)_.
    
- _Advanced Techniques in Metalsmithing_ ([Jewelry Classes](https://canvas.armoryart.org/classes?medium=Jewelry&utm_source=chatgpt.com)).
    

### 🌟 Special Workshops

- _Jewelry-making inspired by Alexander Calder_ (with a Norton Museum tour).
    
- _Ring-making inspired by Anish Kapoor_.
    

**Why Choose Armory?**  
Breadth of classes plus unique museum collaborations make it ideal for anyone wanting to explore across multiple mediums.

---

## **2. Benzaiten Center for Creative Arts (Lake Worth Beach)**

_For the Glass/3D enthusiast_

**Overview**  
Founded in 2015, Benzaiten is a nonprofit fabrication space for three-dimensional and glass-based arts ([Benzaiten Center](https://www.benzaitencenter.org/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)).

### 🎨 Painting

_Not offered_ – Benzaiten focuses exclusively on glass arts.

### 💍 Jewelry

- _Glass Enameling Workshop_ – enamel powders on copper, jewelry creation ($125).
    

### 🔥 Glass Arts

- _Fusing in a Flash_ – beginner cold glass class (ages 12+).
    
- _Fused Glass Tabletop Workshop_ – multi-session project ($475).
    
- Live glassblowing demos & visiting artist programs.
    

**Why Choose Benzaiten?**  
Perfect if you’re drawn to glass as a medium—fusing, enameling, or blown glass. Small groups and community impact (scholarships, school programs) make it accessible and inspiring.

---

## **3. Boca Raton Museum Art School (Boca Raton)**

_For the Jewelry-leaning maker_

**Overview**  
Part of the Boca Raton Museum of Art, the school offers a range of studio-based classes in jewelry, painting, ceramics, mixed media, photography, ikebana, and more ([Boca Art School](https://artschool.bocamuseum.org/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)).

### 🎨 Painting

- _Abstract & Expressionist Painting_.
    
- _Hyperrealism & Portrait Painting_.
    
- _Mixed Media & Collage_.
    
- _Watercolor & Acrylics_.  
    ([Painting Catalog](https://artschool.bocamuseum.org/class-catalog/painting?utm_source=chatgpt.com))
    

### 💍 Jewelry & Metals

- _Jewelry Design & Silversmithing_ – silver & gold, ceremonial objects.
    
- _Advanced Enameling_ – cloisonné, etching, sgraffito.
    
- _Wax Carving for Jewelry_.
    
- _Intro Fashion Jewelry Design_.  
    ([Jewelry Catalog](https://artschool.bocamuseum.org/class-catalog/jewelry?utm_source=chatgpt.com))
    

### 🔥 Glass

_Not offered_ – Boca focuses on jewelry, painting, ceramics, and mixed media.

**Why Choose Boca Art School?**  
Known for depth in jewelry and strong painting programs, all backed by its affiliation with a major museum.
