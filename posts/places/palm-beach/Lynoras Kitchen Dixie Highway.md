Italian kitchen / market-deli vibes on Antique Row — wood-fired pizza, fresh pasta, and weekday lunch deals on South Dixie Highway.

<!--more-->

### Standout specials

- **Meatball Monday** — $3 meatballs, $8 classic martini, $12 Margherita pizza (dine-in).
- **Wine Wednesday** — 1/2 off select bottles.
- **$16.95 lunch specials** (dine-in, 11 AM–3 PM) — soft drink + side; rotates by weekday (pasta or hero).
- **Happy hour every day** — 1/2 off drinks 3–6 PM (7 PM at the bar).
- Daily soup & salad option (minestrone + house or Caesar).

Confirm the live board on [lynoras.com/menu/specials](https://www.lynoras.com/menu/specials/) before you go.
