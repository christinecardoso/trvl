Boynton Beach cocktail kitchen — refined Americana, zero-waste bent, and a cult-favorite house burger. Cafe mornings; lounge nights.

<!--more-->

### Specials & rhythm

- **House burger** — ~8 oz house-ground beef, tangy pickles, rodeo sauce (signature plate; ask if a burger-night deal is running).
- **Sunset Sundays** — Jamaican social / sunset gatherings when on the calendar.
- **Cafe** Wed–Sun mornings/brunch; **dinner lounge** Wed–Sat late (Sun dinner shorter hours).

Closed Mon–Tue evenings typically — check [secondrodeolounge.com](https://www.secondrodeolounge.com/) before you drive.

- **Address:** 640 E Ocean Ave, Suite 6, Boynton Beach, FL 33435
