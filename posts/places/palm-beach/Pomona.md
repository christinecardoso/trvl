Downtown Lake Worth Beach spot from the Ravish team — smash burgers, fried-chicken buckets, and comfort plates with two happy hours a day.

<!--more-->

### Specials

- **Happy hour** daily **3–6 PM** and **late-night 10 PM–close** ($6 margaritas & espresso martinis; select appetizers around $7).
- **Smash burger** on the regular menu (single or double) — American cheese, bacon, herb aioli.
- **Weekend football season** — earlier open (~11:30 AM) with gameday bites when in season.
