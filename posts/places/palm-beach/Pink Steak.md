Modern steakhouse at Belvedere & South Dixie — caviar/raw-bar openers, wood-grilled steaks, and a loud Miami–Palm Beach glam room.

<!--more-->

### Happy hour (Mon–Fri, 4–6 PM)

Bar and high tops only — one drink at a time.

**Drinks**
- Spirits $8 single / $13 double or martini (Stoli, Corazon, Old Forester, Bacardi, New Amsterdam)
- Beers $5 — Amstel Light, Peroni, Modelo, Strongbow, Heineken, Sapporo, Heineken Zero
- Wine by the glass — Notorious Pink Rosé $12 · Stags’ Leap $9 · Y. Rousseau Son of a Butcher $12

**Food**
- All oysters $2 each
- Steak tartare crostini $2.50
- Jumbo shrimp bruschetta $8.50
- Oh My Veggie Roll $10 · Hamachi avocado roll $12

Source: [Happy Hour menu PDF](https://static1.squarespace.com/static/653cf196d38dbe12dec306db/t/673ddaf22cc86c593404067b/1732106995322/Happy+Hour+111924.pdf) (Nov 2024 — confirm prices on arrival).

### Other specials

- **Sunday Lobsta Disco Brunch** — DJ, lobster plates, plus regular brunch menu.
- Signature cocktails and zero-proof list on the full bar menu (Pink Flamingo, Hugo Spritz, etc.).

Dressier than Okeechobee; book ahead for brunch and weekend dinner.
