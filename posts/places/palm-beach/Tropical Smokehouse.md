Florida craft BBQ on South Dixie — pit-smoked meats, Old Fashioned smash burgers, and a full bar. Limited service (order at cashier or QR).

<!--more-->

### Specials

- **Daily happy hour 4:30–5:30 PM** — **$5 single burgers at the bar only**, plus drink specials.
- Does **not** apply to the dining room or takeout.

From [eattropical.com/faq](https://www.eattropical.com/faq). Double burger and full BBQ plates on the regular menu.
